|---|---|---|---|
| `identity.authenticate_active_account` | `with transaction.atomic()` por intento | `LoginAttempt` (`pk=identifier`) -> `Account` (`email_normalized`) | Serializa contador de fallos (`RN-18`), emite `IdentityAuditEvent` y evita carrera `INSERT` con `IntegrityError` + retry |
